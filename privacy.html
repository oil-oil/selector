Privacy Policy

AI Element Picker for Claude & Codex
Last updated: May 2025

Data Collection

AI Element Picker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing activity. The extension operates entirely client-side within your browser.

Permissions

The extension requests access to all URLs solely to allow you to select DOM elements on any webpage you choose. No page content, URLs, or element data is sent to any external server.

Third Parties

No data is shared with any third party. The extension has no backend, no analytics, and no tracking.
